When a trajectory suggests a useful improvement, localize the decision or related
decision set that could have changed the affected behavior. Use actual delivered
inputs, operation results and artifact versions. Keep exposure, execution and
causal interpretation distinct. Missing links stay visible without erasing other
useful observations.

Name the property and the available alternative. A failed task can contain useful
steps, and a successful task can contain harmful or unnecessary work. Review can
repair a defect or reinforce a shared assumption. Do not distribute terminal score
uniformly across the trace or infer utility from counts of checks, reviews or Support.

Trace the explanation through the smallest relevant source spans and later
decisions. Inspect passing contrasts and competing explanations. Retain joint
contributions rather than give every actor the full gain. An alleged cause that
arrives after a change cannot explain that change, although it might affect later
persistence.

Choose a discriminating observation with the offered capabilities. Local artifact
or operation comparisons can isolate a mechanism cheaply. How a model would choose
differently requires an actual context/action intervention and responsive
continuation; general improvement requires fresh task evidence. Preserve the
measured scope separately from those further hypotheses. Use task-authorized
recorded or sandbox state for external-effect comparisons.

Keep quality, cost and latency at their actual scopes. Information is useful when
it improves a consequential decision, not just because another record was produced.
Include research costs. Select experiments that can change the proposed improvement
or adoption decision; no fixed branch count, sample threshold, reset frequency or
total task deadline follows.

Retain attribution as revisable knowledge with decision coordinates, property,
alternative, measured outcome when available, applicability and counterevidence.
Link corrections to earlier claims. Translate useful findings into the smallest
authorized harness/strategy change and bind its actual delivery and measured
outcome before describing adoption. Keep case-specific hidden answers out of
general instructions and live tasks.
